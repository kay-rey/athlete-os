## Why this program (for you)
- **2×/week, ≤48 min** — enough mechanical tension for recomp/LBM without stealing recovery from GB
- **Tue + Fri slots** — Monday recovered slot belongs to GB; Friday after Thu GB uses Zero-Grinder if needed
- **5–8 rep compounds** — RFD + dense “stocky” look (traps/neck, clavicular shelf, lats, anti-lateral core)
- **Trap bar + chest-supported row** — high load, lower lumbar shear for a compact 5'5" grappler
- **Neutral grips / incline press** — spare elbows/shoulders already taxed by gi
- **Neck + carries** — BJJ-specific; most “optimal bodybuilding” splits skip these
- **Not** a bro split or marathon lifting — that overtrains next to GB ≥2×

**Do not add** random third lift days, isolation fluff, or failure sets in Weeks 1–4.

**Sciatica flare rule (he still trains through mild flares):** keep sessions, but **bias load off the nerve** — prefer chest-supported rows over bent-over; shorten DL range / lighter trap-bar; swap back extension → light glute bridge or skip D1 if it lights up the leg. Stop a set if pain shoots down the leg (not just stiff). Kaiser owns weakness / saddle numbness / bowel-bladder change.

**Left thigh asymmetry (locked tapes):** R **22 3/8"** vs L **20 6/8"** (~**−14% CSA**). **Left leg first** on every unilateral set; add **1–2 left-only bonus** sets on Lift B. Prefer DB/KB Bulgarian or step-up over bilateral axial when rebuilding the left.

**Axial load timing:** no heavy trap-bar / squat within **60 min of waking** (incl. after biphasic second sleep) — discs are hydrated and less bend-tolerant. Default noon / evening slots already clear this; if lifting right after nap, extend warm-up + start with unilateral / upper.

## Why not a 3rd lift day?
Your limiting resource is **recovery for GB ≥2× + coaching**, not “more gym stimulus.”

| | 2× MED (default) | Add a 3rd lift day |
|--|------------------|--------------------|
| Strength / LBM in a deficit | High % of what’s recoverable | **Small** extra if sleep/GB stay perfect; often **zero** net if GB quality drops |
| Fatigue | Fits under Thu hard GB + Flow | Stacks CNS/joint tax on elbows/shoulders/spine already used for gi |
| Schedule | Tue/Fri noon | Needs a slot that isn’t Mon/Wed/Thu GB or sleep — usually steals from mats or recovery |
| Tendons (Wk 1–4) | Controlled remodeling | Extra frequency ≠ faster tendon adapt; failure volume raises injury risk |

**Expected gain from +1 day (honest):** maybe a bit more upper-back/leg volume on paper; in practice for a brown belt in a cut with hard GB twice weekly, you’re looking at **marginal strength** (often within noise) and a real chance of **worse Thu/Wed rolls** or nagging elbows. Hard GB already supplies high-tension isometric and pulling work the lift days don’t need to duplicate.

**When to reconsider 3×:** after Phase 1 (or Maintenance) if GB 2× is easy, sleep is solid, waist is moving, and Lift A/B loads are stalling at RPE cap with perfect recovery — then add a short third session, don’t inflate Tue/Fri to 90 min.

## RPE curve (match nutrition phases)

| Phase | Weeks | RPE | RIR | Rule |
|-------|-------|-----|-----|------|
| Tendon / re-sensitize | 1–4 | **6.0–7.0** | 3–4 | Strict tempos + pauses; **no failure** |
| Build | 5–12 | **7.5–8.0** | ~2 | Double-progression adds load |
| Deload | 13 | **6.0–6.5** | ~4 | **2 sets only**, −10–15% load (leptin reset week) |
| Lock-in | 14–21 | **8.0–8.5** | 1–2 | Dense; never grind failed reps |

## Week 1 load finding (Day 1 / first Tue Lift A)
1. Ramp until a set of the **bottom** of the rep range is **RPE ≈6.5–7** (3–4 RIR).  
2. That becomes your working weight for 3 sets.  
3. Log every working set in `journal/lifting.csv` (weight, reps, RPE).  
Milestone waypoints (aspirational) → `athlete/milestones.csv` — score them later; Week 1 is finding the floor.

## Double-progression
Hold weight until you hit the **top** of the rep range on **all 3** working sets inside the phase RPE cap (e.g. trap bar 6/6/6, presses 8/8/8). Next session: **+10 lb** lower (trap bar / squat) or **+5 lb** total upper (usually +2.5/side on DBs).

## Progressive overload & periodization (why this *is* the science-based plan)

You already have both. They just don’t look like a bodybuilding “hypertrophy block → strength block” Instagram template.

### Progressive overload (locked)
**Double-progression** above **is** progressive overload: more reps at a load → then more load. Log every working set. No load jumps outside the RPE cap. That is the primary driver of strength and myofibrillar hypertrophy in a deficit.

### Periodization you already run (undulating + block RPE)
| Weeks | Nutrition phase | Lift emphasis | Science job |
|-------|-----------------|---------------|-------------|
| **1–4** | Phase 1 start | RPE 6–7, tempos, 3–4 RIR | Tendon/collagen catch-up after layoff (Baar); protect joints next to GB |
| **5–12** | Phase 1→2 | RPE 7.5–8, keep adding load | Primary strength + size stimulus (5–8 = both) |
| **13** | Isoenergetic reset | Deload sets/load | Fatigue dissipation + leptin/kcal reset week |
| **14–21** | Phase 4 cut | RPE 8–8.5 | Density / retain LBM while fat drops |

That **is** periodization: intensity and recovery demand change across the 21 weeks in sync with calories — not the same RPE forever.

### Why not separate “hypertrophy then strength” mesocycles?
| Classic BB approach | Why it’s worse *for you* |
|---------------------|---------------------------|
| 8–12+ weeks of 8–15 reps / high volume | Steals recovery from **GB ≥2× + Flow**; more elbow/shoulder junk volume |
| Then a pure 1–5 strength block | In a **cut**, peaking 1RMs is low ROI; BJJ already supplies high-tension isometrics |
| Exercise churn every block | Breaks load tracking on a 2×/week MED |

**5–8 reps at hard loads** sits in the overlap zone: enough tension for hypertrophy, enough load for strength, short enough for a 48-min session. Evidence for concurrent grappler S&C favors **high mechanical tension, low junk volume**, not marathon pump work beside hard sparring.

### What would be *less* scientific here
- Adding a 3rd lift day “for hypertrophy” while GB quality drops  
- Failure sets in Weeks 1–4  
- Ignoring double-progression / not logging loads  
- Linear 5% jumps every week regardless of RPE  
- Aesthetics isolation replacing hinge/press/row/pull-up/neck/carries  

### Maintenance (Wk 22+) — optional later change
After the cut, if GB is easy and you want more size, *then* consider slightly higher reps (6–10) or a short third session — not during Weeks 1–21.
