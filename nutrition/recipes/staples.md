## 1. Chicken tinga bowls
| Exact | Amount |
|-------|--------|
| Chicken breast | **1.5–2 lb** |
| Fire-roasted diced tomatoes | **1–2 cans** (Hunt’s / Kirkland) |
| **La Costeña Chipotle in Adobo** | **1 pepper** + 1 tsp sauce (max 2 if you like heat) |

| Cumin, oregano, salt | To taste |

**Cook:** Simmer 20–25 min stove, shred; or crock Low 5–6 h / pressure ~12 min.  
**Bowl:** tinga + **150g rinsed black beans** (Kirkland Organic) + fajita veg + **1–2 Guerrero/Mission white corn tortillas**. Lime (**no cilantro**).

## 6. Gate 1 — breakfasts (American + Mexican + oatmeal)
Target ~**45g protein**. Brands → `pantry.md`. **Fresca** OK.

### A) Eggs + whites + Mexican mix (**default — best**)
| Exact | Amount |
|-------|--------|
| Whole eggs | **2** |
| Carton egg whites | **1–1.5 cups** |
| Peppers / onion / spinach / zucchini | **Hash-brown substitute** — volume |
| **Kirkland salsa** / pico | Flavor |
| Black beans (optional) | **½ cup** rinsed |
| Corn tortilla | **0–1** if you want a starch side |
| Hash browns / turkey sausage / bacon | **Omit** unless craving |

### B) Oatmeal protein bowl *(weigh everything)*
| Exact | Amount (scale) |
|-------|----------------|
| Rolled oats (or steel-cut), **dry** | **40–60g** (default **50g**) |
| Water | Enough to cook (~**200–250g** water to start; adjust) |
| **ON Gold Standard** (preferred in-bowl) | **~30–32g powder** (1 label scoop ≈ **24P**) — stir in when oats are **warm, not boiling** so it doesn’t clump |
| Extra protein if under ~45P | Add **~150–200g** liquid egg whites into hot oats *or* another **~15–20g** whey |
| **Whole Foods Organic Golden Milk** | His usual **~11g** |
| Banana | **60–120g** edible (½–1 medium) **or** berries **80–100g** |
| Peanut butter (optional) | **16g** (~1 tbsp) if fat room — lift days OK |

**Why whey in the oats:** Yes — that’s the point. Golden milk is flavor/micros; **ON scoop is the Gate 1 protein**. Target bowl **~45g P** (one scoop ≈24P → add whites or a bit more whey).

**Golden milk:** label 3g = 10 kcal · 2C; **~11g ≈ 35–40 kcal · ~7C**. Spices ≠ multi / Tue–Fri Vit C.

**Easy weighed builds:**
1. **Golden whey (default):** **50g** oats + **11g** golden milk + **30–32g** ON + banana **~100g**  
2. **Berry:** **50g** oats + **11g** golden milk + **30–32g** ON + berries **80–100g**  
3. **PB banana:** **50g** oats + **30–32g** ON + banana **60–80g** + PB **16g**  
4. **High protein:** **50g** oats + **32g** ON + **150g** egg whites cooked in + **11g** golden milk

### C) Add turkey sausage or bacon (craving days only)
Eggs/whites build **plus** Jennie-O turkey sausage **2 patties** *or* turkey bacon **2–3** — not stacked with a huge hash every time.

## 7. Gate 3 — chicken + bean lunch bowl
| Exact | ≈ |
|-------|---|
| Chicken (salsa chicken / rotisserie / tinga) | **160–180g cooked** |
| Rinsed black/pinto beans | **Default ½ can** (~125–150g) · **Full can OK** if calories/carbs keep landing low (see below) |
| **Kirkland Organic Medium Salsa** + fajita veg | Volume |
| Avocado | **~30–40g** if fat budget |
| Multi | With lunch |

### One breast at a time (his default)
He cooks **1 breast per cook**. That’s fine — weigh **after** cooking.

| | Typical Costco boneless skinless breast |
|--|----------------------------------------|
| **Raw** | often **~250–340g** (big tray breasts) |
| **Cooked** (~70–75% yield) | often **~180–250g** |
| **Gate plate** | eat **160–180g cooked** |
| **If the breast cooks to ~200–250g** | eat **160–180g** now → fridge the rest for **Gate 4** or dinner add-on |
| **If it’s a small breast (~160–180g cooked)** | eat the whole thing = one Gate |

**Rule:** **1 cooked breast ≈ 1 Gate protein** when it’s in the 160–180g band; oversized breasts = **one plate + leftover**, not force the whole bird down. Air fryer 375°F ~12–16 min to **165°F**, or oven 400°F ~18–22 min, or stove 5–7 min/side.

### Beans when under on carbs/calories
He’s been finishing days **~400–700 under**, mostly carbs/fat short while protein is fine.

| Pattern | When |
|---------|------|
| **½ can lunch + ½ can dinner** | Gut sensitive / default |
| **1 full drained can lunch + 1 full drained can dinner** (~**2 cans/day** rinsed black or pinto) | **Allowed** while under-eating is the failure mode — ~**+660 kcal · ~118C · ~44 fiber** from beans alone |
| **Refried** | Prefer **not** 2 full cans — denser fat/Na; use rinsed for the 2-can pattern |

**Rules:** rinse well · keep psyllium at Wk1 **½ tsp** (don’t stack more gel) · if bloated/gassy → drop to **1½ cans/day** or half+full · still hit chicken **160–180g** so protein isn’t only beans · log drained amount honestly.

## 8. Quick sandwiches (Gate 3 lunch or tired Gate 6)
**Rules:** **no pickles** · protein first (~**45–60g**) · **1 starch** (2 bread slices **or** wrap — not both + chips) · salsa / Hellmann’s Light / ketchup / BBQ OK · mustard OK on turkey/chicken (only skip mustard on **burgers/dogs**). Side: apple, carrots, or **½–1 cup rinsed beans** — not chips.  
**Bread:** **2 slices** — Carb Balance OK if it keeps you fuller without GI blowback; soft WW/white if it doesn’t (`../preferences.md`).

| # | Build | Exact | ≈ | When |
|---|-------|-------|---|------|
| **1 — Best default** | **Turkey breast sandwich** | Soft bread **2 slices** · **4–6 oz** sliced turkey breast (or ~120–160g) · lettuce/tomato/onion · **Kirkland salsa** or **1 tbsp Hellmann’s Light** | ~400–500 · **45–55P** · 35–45C · 8–12F | Fastest Gate 3 |
| **2 — Higher protein** | **Double turkey** | Same as #1 with **6–8 oz** turkey · easy on mayo | ~450–550 · **55–70P** | Protein behind by 5pm |
| **3 — Rotisserie** | **Chicken sandwich** | **4–6 oz** Kirkland rotisserie (skin off) · 2 slices bread · salsa or light mayo · lettuce/tomato | ~400–500 · **45–55P** | Leftover bird |
| **4 — BBQ** | **BBQ chicken sandwich** | Same chicken · **2 tbsp G Hughes SF Sweet & Spicy** · 2 slices · slaw only if **no pickle** vinegar bomb | ~400–480 · **45–55P** | American night |
| **5 — Egg** | **Egg-white scramble sandwich** | 2 slices · **1 cup egg whites + 1 whole egg** cooked · salsa · spinach | ~350–420 · **40–50P** | Breakfast-for-lunch |
| **6 — Open-face / lighter carb** | **One-slice open-face** | **1 slice** bread · **5–6 oz** turkey/chicken · big lettuce/tomato · salsa | ~300–400 · **45–55P** · fewer C | Carb budget tight |
| **7 — Tortilla “sandwich”** | **Corn tortilla stack** | **2 Guerrero/Mission corn tortillas** · turkey/chicken · salsa · optional ½ cup beans | ~350–450 · **40–50P** | Milpa lean |
| **8 — Deli / Subway-style** | **Custom sub** | **6" turkey or grilled chicken** · wheat/white · load veg · **no pickles** · light mayo or mustard OK · skip oil/cheese lake · no bacon default | aim **45P+** | Out of house — ask no pickles |
| **Occasional** | Thin cheese **1 slice** on #1–3 | — | +~50–70 kcal · +4–6F | Fat allows |
| **Skip** | Pickle spears · chips as the side · double bread + wrap + fries · mayo lakes · salami/pepperoni stacks · “just cheese” | — | Binge / fat / pickle traps |

**Assembly time:** ~5 min if turkey/rotisserie is in the fridge. Batch-slice turkey Sunday with Costco chicken.

**Lose It:** log bread brand + weighed meat. Deli meat varies — prefer **oven-roasted turkey breast** low sugar, not honey-glazed candy turkey.
