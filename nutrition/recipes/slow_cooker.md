# Slow cooker / multi-cooker staples

For low-cook nights (he dislikes cooking; wife sometimes tired). Taste → `nutrition/preferences.md`. Chili deep-dive → `turkey_chili.md`. Portions: Phase defaults (moderate potato/noodle, lean protein).

**Appliance:** Instant Pot–style multi-cooker on **Slow** (or pressure notes where listed). ~6 qt.

**Shared rules**
- Lean protein + low-sodium broth / no-salt tomatoes + rinsed beans
- **Don’t** dump cream soup, cheese packets, or 80/20 meat
- Noodles: add **last 20–30 min** on warm/high, or cook separate and portion into bowls
- Freeze leftovers in single-serve containers (tired-night backup)

---

## 1. Turkey chili (easiest win)
Full macros → `turkey_chili.md`. Crock version:

| In the pot | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| **99/1** ground turkey | **2 lb** (brown first in skillet or sauté mode — optional but better texture; or break up raw in pot) |
| Black/pinto beans, rinsed | **2 cans** (15 oz) |
| Diced tomatoes, no-salt-added | **2 cans** (14.5 oz) |
| Chili seasoning | **½–1 packet** + cumin/chili powder/garlic |
| Onion (optional) | 1, chopped |

**Cook:** Low **6–7 h** or High **3–4 h**. Stir once if you remember.  
**Bowl:** ~160g meat + 150g beans (see chili file). Freeze extras.

## 2. Chicken tortilla soup (best Milpa pot)
| In the pot | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| Chicken breast (or thighs trimmed) | **1.5–2 lb** |
| Low-sodium chicken broth | **4–6 cups** |
| Diced tomatoes | **1–2 cans** |
| Black beans, rinsed | **1–2 cans** |
| Frozen corn (optional) | **1 cup** |
| Onion + garlic | 1 onion, 3 cloves |
| Chili powder, cumin, oregano, salt | To taste |
| Lime + cilantro | Finish at serving |

**Cook:** Low **6–7 h** or High **3–4 h**. Shred chicken in pot; stir.  
**Serve:** Bowl of soup + **few** baked corn tortilla strips (or 1 warm tortilla on the side if carbs allow). Light avocado OK. Skip sour cream lake.  
**Pressure (multi-cooker):** ~12–15 min high pressure + natural release 10 min, then shred.

**Target bowl:** ~45–55P · beans for carbs · not a chip mountain.

---

## 3. Salsa chicken (lowest effort — builds bowls all week)
| In the pot | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| Chicken breast | **2 lb** |
| Jar salsa (no weird cream) | **16–24 oz** |
| Cumin / chili powder | Optional pinch |

**Cook:** Low **5–6 h** or High **3 h**. Shred; keep in juices.  
**Use:** Over beans + veg; with 1–2 corn tortillas; into tortilla soup leftover broth; egg scramble add-in.  
**Freeze** in 150–200g packs ≈ Gate protein.

---

## 4. Chicken noodle soup (noodles late)
| In the pot | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| Chicken breast or rotisserie leftover | **1.5 lb** (or meat from 1 bird, skin off) |
| Low-sodium broth | **6–8 cups** |
| Carrot, celery, onion | Generous |
| Garlic, bay, pepper, salt | To taste |
| Potato (optional) | **1 medium**, cubed — **or** skip if doing noodles |
| Egg noodles | **~4–6 oz dry for whole pot** (~1–1.5 oz dry per big bowl) — **add last 20–30 min** |

**Cook:** Low **6–7 h** (chicken + veg + broth). Shred chicken. Add noodles last.  
**Phase default:** protein + veg heavy; **moderate** noodles — don’t refill the pot with pasta. Prefer potato **or** noodle, not both piled.

---

## 5. Albóndigas-style (turkey meatballs + potato)
Slightly more work (form balls once). Batch Sunday.

| Meatballs | Broth pot |
|-----------|-----------|
| **2 lb 99/1 turkey** + minced onion/garlic + handful rice or masa + salt/cumin/oregano; roll golf-ball size | Low-sodium broth **6 cups** + **1 medium potato** cubed + carrot/zucchini/tomato + cilantro |

**Cook:** Drop raw or lightly browned meatballs into simmering broth in cooker; Low **5–6 h** or High **3–4 h**.  
**Bowl:** Several meatballs + veg + **one** potato portion (~medium across servings — don’t load 2 potatoes per person).

---

## 6. Fideos note
Classic fideo wants a toast + short simmer — **awkward as pure slow-cook**. Better: make tomato-chicken broth in the crock (chicken + tomatoes + onion + broth all day), then **toast fideo in a pan 5 min** and simmer in a ladle of broth at dinner — **1–1.5 oz dry per bowl**. Or skip crock and use pressure “soup” then finish noodles separate.

## Weekly rhythm (minimal cook)
1. **Sun or one morning:** chili **or** tortilla soup **or** salsa chicken in the cooker.  
2. Freeze half. Fridge half.  
3. Tired night = thaw/microwave — no decisions.  
4. Rotate: chili → tortilla → salsa chicken → chicken noodle.

Costco/Sam’s pulls: 99/1 turkey, chicken breasts, broth cartons, beans, tomatoes, salsa, frozen veg, potatoes, egg noodles/fideo.

## What else the multi-cooker is good for (beyond those pots)

Use **pressure** when you want food in ~15–40 min without standing at the stove. Times vary by model — start with the manual’s bean/poultry charts.

### High-value for *this* OS
| Use | Why it helps you | How (rough) |
|-----|------------------|---------------|
| **Dried black/pinto beans** | Cheaper + lower sodium than cans; Milpa staple | Soak optional; pressure ~25–35 min + natural release; salt after. Freeze in 1-cup packs. |
| **Chicken from frozen** | Forgot to thaw — still get dinner protein | Pressure breast/thighs w/ salsa or broth ~12–18 min; shred. |
| **Big batch shredded chicken/turkey** | Lunch + tired nights without cooking | Same as salsa chicken but pressure; portion + freeze. |
| **Chicken tinga-style** | Mexican flavor you like, dump-friendly | Chicken + tomatoes/chipotle-in-adobo (go easy — spicy) + onion; shred; serve w/ beans + 1–2 tortillas. |
| **Potato batch** | Controlled starch for soups/sides | Cubed potatoes steam/pressure ~5–8 min; fridge for the week — scoop **one** medium’s worth. |
| **Hard-boiled eggs** | Gate breakfast/lunch protein, almost no cook skill | Steamer rack + 1 cup water; pressure ~5–6 min + ice bath. |
| **Steel-cut oats** | Late-wake breakfast if you want hot carbs | Pressure ~4–5 min + release; portion; top protein (egg whites / WPI stirred cool). |
| **Bone-ish broth / leftover carcass** | Cheap soup base, low effort | Rotisserie bones + onion/carrot/celery + water; slow overnight or pressure ~45–60 min; skim fat. |
| **Steam frozen veg** | Volume under protein when she’s tired | Steam mode / pot-in-pot — don’t deep-fry the night away. |
| **Reheat chili/soup** | Better than nuking dry (optional) | Slow/warm or sauté with splash of broth — microwave is fine too. |

### Fine sometimes
- **Rice** — OK peri / Phase 3; don’t make rice the default dinner carb over beans/potato/tortilla.
- **Lean pot roast / flank-style shred** — sirloin tip or similar + salsa/broth; not fatty chuck every week.
- **Egg white “bites”** (silicone molds, pot-in-pot) — only if you’ll actually eat them; otherwise eggs in a pan is simpler.

### Skip / low priority for you
- Yogurt maker (you want **lactose-free** dairy — store lactose-free Greek is easier)
- Cheesecake, cakes, “set it and forget dessert”
- Creamy pasta dumps, ribs candy sauces, cream-of-X casseroles
- Anything that needs babysitting → defeats the point

### Simple weekly add-ons (pick 1–2, not all)
1. Pressure a bag of **dried beans** → fridge/freezer.  
2. Pressure **salsa chicken** or tinga → protein packs.  
3. **Egg batch** once if breakfasts are slipping.  
4. Keep using **slow mode** for chili / tortilla soup on days someone’s home to dump ingredients.
