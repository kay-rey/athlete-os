# Turkey chili (staple recipe)

Liked meal deep-dive. Taste rules → `nutrition/preferences.md`. Macros → `nutrition/phases.csv`. Gates → `day/schedule.md`.

## Your build
Ground turkey + beans + chili mix packet + canned tomatoes. Fits **American + bean/Milpa** rules. No pickle conflict.

## Meat choice — default **99/1 turkey**; lean beef OK as a swap
Phase 1 fat cap is **72g**. Chili tomatoes + beans keep ultra-lean meat moist.

| Per 100g cooked (drained) | kcal | P | F | Chili role |
|---------------------------|------|---|---|------------|
| **99/1 turkey** (default) | ~151 | ~32 | **~2.7** | Best protein-per-fat |
| **96/4 beef** (best beef) | ~153 | ~28.5 | **~3.8** | Closest beef match to 99/1 |
| 93/7 beef | ~209 | ~29 | **~9.5** | OK occasionally; costs fat budget |
| 93/7 turkey | ~213 | ~27 | **~11.6** | Fallback if 99/1 gone |
| 90/10 beef | ~230 | ~28.5 | **~11** | Same problem as 93/7 turkey |
| 80/20 / 85/15 beef | ~250+ | ~26–27 | **~15+** | Skip for Phase chili |

**Same ~50g meat protein in a Gate bowl (meat only):**
- 99/1 turkey (~160g): **~4g fat**
- 96/4 beef (~175g): **~7g fat**
- 93/7 beef (~173g): **~16g fat**
- 80/20 beef (~185g): **~28g fat** — blows the day if you also want avocado/fish oil

Beef chili is fine American variety (iron/B12 bump). Prefer **96/4** when swapping; **93/7 max** otherwise. Don’t make 80/20 the weekly default.

## Ingredient facts (typical)
| Ingredient | Reference | Per amount |
|------------|-----------|------------|
| **99/1** ground turkey, cooked | USDA fat-free crumbles | **100g:** 151 kcal · 31.7P · 0C · 2.7F |
| **96/4** ground beef (swap) | label / lean estimates | **100g cooked ≈** 153 kcal · 28.5P · 0C · 3.8F |
| 93/7 beef or turkey (fallback) | USDA | beef ~209 kcal · 29P · 9.5F; turkey ~213 · 27P · 11.6F |
| Black/pinto beans, canned, drained+rinsed | USDA | **100g:** ~115 kcal · 7P · 20C · 1.3F · ~200mg Na |
| Diced tomatoes, canned | USDA | **100g:** 18 kcal · 0.8P · 3.3C · 0.5F · ~125mg Na (higher if not “no salt added”) |
| Chili seasoning mix (e.g. McCormick Original 1.25 oz pkt) | Label | **9g (1⅓ tbsp ≈ 1 cup chili):** 25 kcal · ~5C · 2 fiber · **340–350mg Na**; ingredients: chili pepper/spices, **enriched wheat flour**, onion, salt, garlic (± sugar on some lots) |

**Watch-outs:** Packet mix is **sodium-dense** and often contains **wheat flour** (not GF). Prefer **no-salt-added tomatoes** + **rinse beans** to offset. If a packet is very salty, use **½–1 packet per 2 lb turkey** and boost flavor with cumin, chili powder, garlic, onion powder, oregano.

## Phase 1 plate (Gate 3 lunch **or** Gate 5/6 dinner) — one bowl
Target ≈ Gate protein (~50g) + bean carbs. Numbers below assume **99/1**.

| Component | Amount | ≈ Macros |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| **99/1** turkey, cooked | **160g** (≈50P) | 242 kcal · 51P · 0C · 4F |
| Beans, drained | **150g** | 173 kcal · 10P · 30C · 2F |
| Diced tomatoes (with liquid OK) | **200g** | 36 kcal · 2P · 7C · 1F |
| Chili mix | **~1 label serving (9g)** | 25 kcal · 0P · 5C · 0.5F · ~350mg Na |
| **Bowl total** | | **≈ 476 kcal · 63P · 42C · 8F** |

**Phase 1 daily budget hit (2,250 / 185P / 215C / 72F):** ~21% kcal · ~34% protein · ~20% carbs · **~11% fat** — leaves fat room for avocado / fish oil. Pair with multi at lunch if Gate 3; light crema optional.

## Batch cook (Sun) — ~4 Gate bowls
- **2.0 lbs** raw **99/1 turkey** (default) **or 96/4 beef** (swap); browned — drain beef well; splash water/broth if turkey sticks
- **2 cans** (15 oz) black or pinto beans, **rinsed**
- **2 cans** (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes — **no salt added** if available
- **1 packet McCormick Original Chili Seasoning** (stretch with extra cumin/chili powder rather than 2 full packets unless you need the salt) — beans/tomatoes brands → `pantry.md`
- Optional: onion, bell pepper, jalapeño for volume

**Slow cooker / multi-cooker:** same ingredients — Low **6–7 h** or High **3–4 h**. Full dump list → [`slow_cooker.md`](slow_cooker.md).

Store 3–4 days fridge or freeze. Reheat for Gate 3 or post-mat dinner. Tortilla on the side only if carbs remain that day.
