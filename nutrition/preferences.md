## Snacks — Spitz Cracked Pepper seeds (deep dive)
**SKU:** Spitz® Cracked Pepper Flavored Sunflower Seeds (Frito-Lay / PepsiCo) — common **6 oz** bag; UPC often **028400096720**. In-shell; you crack & spit shells (typical drive habit).

| Label serving | ≈ **28g** edible (“About 3 Tbsp hulled seeds with seasoning”) |
|---------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| Calories | **170** |
| Fat | **13g** (sat **2g**) |
| Carb | **5g** (fiber **3g**, sugar ~1g) |
| Protein | **6g** |
| Sodium | **~610mg** |
| Servings / 6 oz bag | **~3.5** → whole bag ≈ **~600 kcal · ~45F · ~21P · ~2100mg Na** |

**Ingredients (typical):** sunflower seeds; seasoning (sea salt, salt, corn maltodextrin, yeast extract, black pepper, citric acid, onion, garlic, sugar, gum arabic, natural flavor); vegetable oil. No dairy → **lactose OK**. Not a pickle/mustard conflict.

**Quality:** Standard flavored seed snack — fine food, not a “superfood.” Fat is mostly seed oil (OK type); **problem is dose**. Label serving is **kernels**, not “handful of whole shells” — easy to under-log while driving.

**Phase fit (2,250 / 185P / 215C / 72F):**
- **1 label serving** = ~**18% of daily fat** for only **6g protein** — expensive fat for weak protein
- Sodium high per serving; you have **no hard Na cap** (peri needs salt) — still don’t stack a whole bag + salty soup night mindlessly
- Fiber bump (+3g) is minor vs 45g target

| Strategy | Rule |
|----------|------|
| **Phase default** | **≤1 serving (28g kernels / ~⅓ of a small bag’s edible)** on drive days — pre-portion or stop when shells pile ≈ that serving |
| **Better** | Buy **smaller bags** or portion into a snack bag before the drive; log in Lose It as Spitz Cracked Pepper |
| **Not** | Whole 6 oz bag as “just seeds while driving” (~half+ of fat budget) |
| **Role** | Oral-habit / road snack — OK occasional; **does not replace Gate protein** |

**Verdict:** Keep the flavor you like. Treat as a **budgeted fat snack**, not free volume food. Prefer **1 serving**, not bag-finishing.

Milpa rule: 65–75% non-peri carbs from beans/tortillas/veg. Potato beats refined noodle for satiety/fiber; beans beat both. Prefer that order in advice even when he likes noodle piles.

## Wraps — “zero net carb” / low-carb tortillas (deep dive)
Common SKUs: **Mission® Zero Net Carbs** (Original), similar “carb balance” / keto wraps. Marketing = carbs − fiber ≈ 0 (“net carbs”).

| | Mission Zero Net Carbs (typ. 1 wrap ~40g) | Nixtamal **corn** tortilla (~1 small) |
|--|------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Label calories | ~**80–110** | ~**50–60** |
| Total carb / fiber | ~**14–19g** / ~**14–19g** → “0 net” | ~**10–12g** / ~**1–2g** |
| Protein | ~**5–7g** | ~**1–2g** |
| Main ingredients | Modified starch, **wheat gluten**, cellulose, shortening | **Corn** (lime-treated) |
| Milpa? | **No** | **Yes** |

**Reality check:** “Zero net” ≠ calorie-free and ≠ blood-sugar-free. Some modified starches still raise glucose; cellulose fiber is gut filler. You already use **psyllium** — stacking mega-fiber wraps can mean gas/bloating.

**Phase fit (215g carbs):** You are **not** on keto. You *need* real carbs (beans, corn, potato, peri). Swapping every tortilla for zero-net wraps does **not** improve recomp and weakens Milpa.

| Strategy | Rule |
|----------|------|
| **Healthiest default** | **Nixtamalized corn tortillas** 1–2 with bowls/fajitas |
| **Zero-net wraps** | OK **sometimes** when you want a big wrap format and are saving carbs for potato/noodle soup night — log as the label (total carbs + fiber), not “free food” |
| **Not** | Daily “unlimited wraps because zero net”; don’t count them as Milpa |
| **If GI complains** | Drop the wraps; keep corn + beans + psyllium schedule |

**Verdict:** Keep them as a **liked option**, not the staple. **Corn tortillas win** for health + Phase. Zero-net = convenience wrap, not a upgrade.

## Soups — healthy to make? (yes — Phase defaults)

Homemade > restaurant/canned. All four fit taste. **Healthiest pattern:** lean protein + veg volume + **moderate** potato *or* noodle (not both piled), low-sodium broth. Taste likes heavy potato/noodle — **do not prescribe heavy as the default**.

### Shared Phase rules (tell wife / batch cook)
- Broth: **low-sodium** chicken broth + salt to taste (or no-salt tomatoes + cumin/chili/oregano/lime)
- Protein: **lean and generous** — chicken breast/thigh trimmed, **99/1 turkey** or **96/4** meatballs; drain fat. Target ~**45–60g protein** / bowl
- Veg: onion, garlic, carrot, celery, zucchini, tomato, cilantro — **this is the volume**, not extra pasta
- Starch: **potato preferred over noodle** when choosing one; Phase default = moderate (table); heavy = occasional, not nightly
- Finish: lime, cilantro, salsa; light avocado if fat allows; skip sour cream lakes / cheese dumps
- Costco/Sam’s: rotisserie (skin off), broth, frozen veg, potatoes, fideo/egg noodles, 99/1 turkey

| In the bowl | Carbs (approx) | Agent default |
|-------------|----------------|---------------|
| **Potato** ~1 medium (~150–170g cooked) | ~**30–35g** | **Yes — Phase default** for albóndigas / chicken soups |

**Verdict:** Make these soups. Prefer **protein + veg + one moderate starch** (potato > noodle; beans when it fits). Enjoy potato/noodle flavors without making “heavy” the prescription.

### Homemade “shaved ice” (blender OK)
A blender **works** for the craving, but it makes **crushed / snow-cone ice**, not true fluffy Hawaiian powder (that needs a shave-ice blade). Close enough for Phase goals.

**Method**
1. Freeze water in trays; use **hard, dry ice** (not wet).
2. Pulse in a strong blender or food processor — **short pulses**, scrape sides; don’t let it melt into a smoothie. Work in small batches. Optional: chill the blender jar in the freezer 10 min.
3. Pack into a bowl/cup; drizzle syrup **lightly** (thin SF syrup sinks fast — pour in layers while packing ice).
4. Eat immediately.

**Phase syrup (prefer this nightly/anytime)**
- **Sugar-free Torani / Jordan’s / similar** (Splenda or sucralose), or SF Hawaiian shaved-ice syrup — ~**0 kcal**
- DIY: unsweetened Kool-Aid packet + granulated erythritol/Splenda + water + **tiny pinch xanthan gum** (helps cling; blend briefly)
- **Fresca pour-over:** pack blender ice, splash cold Fresca — grapefruit “soda ice” you already like

**Skip for free version:** regular syrup, condensed milk, ice cream. Those go back to the 1×/week budgeted treat.
