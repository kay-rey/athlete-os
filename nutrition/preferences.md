## Soups — healthy to make? (yes, with these builds)

Homemade > restaurant/canned. All four fit Mexican/American lean tastes. You **like potato- and noodle-heavy bowls** — that’s fine; don’t “diet soup” them into thin broth. Main risks: **fatty meatballs**, **salty broth**, **fried-tortilla/cheese mountains**, and **no protein under the starch**.

### Shared Phase rules (tell wife / batch cook)
- Broth: **low-sodium** chicken broth + salt to taste (or no-salt tomatoes + cumin/chili/oregano/lime)
- Protein: **lean and generous** — chicken breast/thigh trimmed, **99/1 turkey** or **96/4** for meatballs; drain fat. Starch-heavy bowl still needs ~**45–60g protein** so you don’t graze later
- Veg: onion, garlic, carrot, celery, zucchini, tomato, cilantro — pile on (volume under the potato/noodle)
- **Potato + noodle (your preference):** keep them — they’re the **dinner carb slot** that night
- Finish: lime, cilantro, salsa; **avocado** if fat budget; skip sour cream lakes / giant cheese dumps (lactose + fat)
- Costco/Sam’s: rotisserie chicken (skin off), broth cartons, frozen veg, potatoes, egg noodles / fideo, 99/1 turkey

## Desserts — shaved ice (deep dive)
Typical shop cup (~1 cup ice + syrup): ≈ **180–250 kcal · 0P · 40–55C · 0F**, almost all **sugar/HFCS**. Optional condensed milk / ice cream = +fat + lactose (skip or go light — lactase non-persistence).

| Strategy | Rule |
|----------|------|
| **Phase 1 default** | **0–1× / week**, after protein-by-5pm is hit; log in Lose It |
| **Budget** | Pull ~**40–50g** from daily **215g carbs** (swap out tortilla/rice/peri fluff that day) |
| **Order** | Small/kids size; **one syrup**; skip condensed milk / mochi / ice cream cap |
| **Better fit** | Sugar-free syrup shaved ice at home ≈ **0–20 kcal** — free like Fresca if you like the texture |
| **Not** | Nightly dessert or “I trained so I earned a large + cream” |

**Verdict:** Keep as a **liked treat**, not a staple. Texture craving → sugar-free syrup ice or Fresca + ice first.

### Homemade “shaved ice” (blender OK)
A blender **works** for the craving, but it makes **crushed / snow-cone ice**, not true fluffy Hawaiian powder (that needs a shave-ice blade). Close enough for Phase goals.

**Method**
1. Freeze water in trays; use **hard, dry ice** (not wet).
2. Pulse in a strong blender or food processor — **short pulses**, scrape sides; don’t let it melt into a smoothie. Work in small batches. Optional: chill the blender jar in the freezer 10 min.
3. Pack into a bowl/cup; drizzle syrup **lightly** (thin SF syrup sinks fast — pour in layers while packing ice).
4. Eat immediately.

**Phase syrup (prefer this nightly/anytime)**
- **Sugar-free Torani / Jordan’s / similar** (Splenda or sucralose), or SF Hawaiian shaved-ice syrup — ~**0 kcal**
- DIY: unsweetened Kool-Aid packet + granulated erythritol/Splenda + water + **tiny pinch xanthan gum** (helps cling; blend briefly)
- **Fresca pour-over:** pack blender ice, splash cold Fresca — grapefruit “soda ice” you already like

**Skip for free version:** regular syrup, condensed milk, ice cream. Those go back to the 1×/week budgeted treat.

## Dislikes (hard avoid)
- **Pickles / pickle relish** — despise; never put on plates, burgers, bowls, or “add pickles for volume”
- **Super vinegary foods** — skip pickle brine, vinegar-forward slaws, heavy vinegar hot sauces as the main flavor, vinegar-heavy BBQ (Carolina-style). Mild salsa acidity / citrus lime is OK unless you say otherwise
- **Mustard** — don’t default to it; use salsa, guac, light mayo/crema, BBQ, or cheese sparingly instead
