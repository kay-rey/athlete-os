## Success tiers (use these in Sunday audits)

### Tier A — Stage 1 floor (still a win @ Wk 21)
- Weight ≈ **172–176 lbs** (point band **172–174.5**)
- Waist **≤37"** (prefer **36.5–37.0"** / ~**−4"**)
- BF roughly **~21–23%** directionally down
- LBM **preserved** (+ ICW OK)
- A1 jacket closes; pants ≤ size 33
- Training consistency ≥80%; no injury from grinding
- **Did not** chronically undereat &lt;2,150 kcal

### Tier B — Stage 1 strong / Stage 2 ready
- Weight **172–174.5** with waist **~36.5–36.8"**
- Clean bridge into Stage 2 toward **166 / 33–34"**
- LBM holding ~134-ish with creatine + training
- Strength progressing under the RPE curve; gi overlap ≥4"

### Tier C — Goal anchor (post–Stage 2)
- **164–168 lb** (point **166**), waist **33–34"**, ~19% BF, ~134.5 FFM
- Dense Lightweight-frame brown belt — usually needs the **8-wk Stage 2** after the post–Wk 21 bridge, not a deeper cut now

## Practical rule for this repo
- Keep `athlete/milestones.csv` as the **aspirational dashboard** (Stage 1 anthro + strength aims).
- Score weekly progress against **Tier A / Stage 1** as the non-negotiable floor.
- Do **not** deepen the deficit or add random volume if LBM/stockiness lags while waist and adherence are good.
- Weeks 1–3: waist > scale. Weeks 14–21: expect to look flatter before denser.
- **Central / chest fat pattern:** belly inches first; chest soft tissue later. No spot-reduction volume or crash diets for man-boobs.

## Bottom line
**Yes — ~173 lb / ~37" waist and a tighter gi by Week 21 is the realistic Stage 1 win.**  
**No — 166 lb / 33–34" is the Stage 2 finish line, not a single 21-week photograph.**
