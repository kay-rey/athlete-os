# Trajectory realism

Numbers/dates → `profile.md` + `milestones.csv`. This file owns **Tier framing only**.

Week-21 “stocky lock-in” is **best-case**, not guaranteed. Use tiers so a good outcome isn’t scored as failure.

## What is realistic in 21 weeks

| Component | Verdict | Why |
|-----------|---------|-----|
| **Fat loss (~20–25 lbs FM)** | **Realistic** if adherence is high | Bigger start FM (~55+ vs old ~50) = same weekly fat-loss rate, **more total runway**; kcal plan unchanged |
| **Scale 189.7 → ~170–174** | **Realistic as direction** | Mostly fat + early ECW/gut; Wk 1–3 ICW stall. Hitting 172 @ ~15% needs more LBM gain than median — score composition by waist |
| **Waist ~40–41" → ≤35–36" (Tier A) / 31–33" (Tier B)** | **Tier A plausible; Tier B aggressive** | ~5–6" drop in 21 wks is a strong win; 31.5" from ~41" is a stretch, not a fail if missed |
| **A1 gi closes / overlap ↑** | **Realistic** | Follows waist + torso fat loss even before full LBM refill |
| **Full +6.2 lbs net LBM inside the deficit** | **Optimistic** | Muscle-memory rebound after a 1-yr layoff makes *some* concurrent recomp real; banking the entire +6.2 lbs *while* losing ~24 lbs fat is best-case, not median |
| **“Stocky” dense look by Wk 21** | **Usually needs longer** | Lean + tighter by Wk 21 is the right expectation. Thick neck/trap yoke, full clavicular shelf, and “hard” muscle fullness typically consolidate in **Wk 22–30+ maintenance** at ~2,500 kcal when glycogen/ICW refill without a deficit |
| **Strength milestones (e.g. 335×5 trap bar)** | **Conditional** | Fast return toward *prior* working maxes is normal with myonuclear memory. If those loads were never near your pre-layoff ceiling, treat them as Phase-4 *aims*, not pass/fail |

## Success tiers (use these in Sunday audits)

### Tier A — Floor (still a win)
- Weight ≈ **176–180 lbs** by Wk 21
- Waist ≤ **35–36"** (or **−5"** from locked AM baseline)
- BF roughly **~18–22%** (directionally down)
- LBM **preserved or +1–3 lbs**
- A1 jacket closes; pants ≤ size 33
- Training consistency ≥80%; no injury from grinding

### Tier B — Plan target (playbook primary — stretch from ~41")
- Weight **170–174 lbs**
- Waist **31–33"**
- BF ≈ **15–18%**
- LBM **+3–6 lbs** net (partial fill; harder if true start LBM is ~131)
- Strength progressing under the RPE curve; gi overlap ≥4–5"

### Tier C — Stocky lock-in (often post-deficit)
- Tier B bodyweight/waist **plus**
- Visible yoke / clavicular shelf / lat thickness that reads “stocky grappler”
- Usually requires **4–8 weeks at Maintenance (2,500 kcal)** after Wk 21 for glycogen, training quality, and regional hypertrophy to show

## Practical rule for this repo
- Keep `athlete/milestones.csv` as the **aspirational dashboard** (Tier B aiming at Tier C).
- Score weekly progress against **Tier A as the non-negotiable floor**.
- Do **not** deepen the deficit or add random volume if LBM/stockiness lags while waist and adherence are good — that is when you hold the plan and let Maintenance finish the look.
- Weeks 1–3: waist > scale. Weeks 14–21: expect to look flatter before you look denser.
- **Central / chest fat pattern:** expect **belly inches first**, chest soft tissue later (often mid–late Phase + maintenance). Do not add pec isolation volume or crash the deficit to “spot-reduce” man-boobs.

## Bottom line
**Yes — getting meaningfully leaner, tighter in the gi, and stronger in 21 weeks is a realistic goal.**  
**No — the finished “stocky brown-belt build” is more often a ~6–8 month arc (21-week cut/recomp + dedicated maintenance fill), not a single 21-week photograph.**
