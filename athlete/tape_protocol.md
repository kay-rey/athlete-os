# Tape protocol — accurate navel waist (SoT method)

**Phase progress SoT = navel waist**, not scale. Targets → `athlete/profile.md`. Log → `journal/anthro.csv`.  
Chat convention: `N.k` = **N + k/8 inches** (e.g. `40.2` → 40 2/8"), not decimal tenths — unless he explicitly says “decimal.”

## When to measure
| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Default** | **Sunday AM** audit (with weekly weigh-in) |
| **Also OK** | Any morning you want a check — same conditions |
| **Time** | **Within 30 min of waking**, after bathroom, **before** food/coffee/creatine shake |
| **Not** | Post-meal, post-GB, post-Lift, evening, or dehydrated/sauna day |
| **Cadence** | **1×/week minimum** (Sunday). Extra checks OK; don’t tape 5×/day |

## Setup
1. **Same tape every time** (flexible cloth/vinyl sewing tape — not a metal carpenter tape).  
2. **Skin** — shirt up; tape on bare skin at navel (not over a shirt).  
3. **Stance** — feet together or ~hip-width, weight even, standing tall, arms relaxed at sides.  
4. **Mirror or phone front camera** optional to check level.

## Landmark (lock this)
1. Find the **center of the navel** (belly button).  
2. Tape runs in a **horizontal ring** through that point — level all the way around.  
3. **Not** the narrowest waist, **not** pants waistband, **not** above/below by “feel.”  
4. Optional: small ink mark at navel height on the side for fast re-find (washable).

## Tape tension (the usual error)
1. Pull snug so the tape **lies flat** and doesn’t sag.  
2. **Not** digging a trench / compressing fat.  
3. **Not** loose with gaps.  
4. Check: you can slip a fingertip under the tape with light resistance — same feel every week.

## Breathing
1. Breathe normally.  
2. Read at **end of a normal exhale** — not sucked in, not fully inflated belly.  
3. No “contest vacuum.” No holding a big breath.

## Read & record
1. Look at the tape where it meets — read to the nearest **1/8 inch**.  
2. Do **2 measures** (step away, reset tape, repeat).  
3. If they differ by **≤0.25" (2/8")**, log the **average**.  
4. If they differ by **>0.25"**, do a **3rd** — log the average of the closest two.  
5. Tell the agent: e.g. `navel 40.2` (eighths) or `navel 40 2/8` · optional photo of tape at navel.

## Log fields (`journal/anthro.csv`)
- `date`, `time_local` (morning), `weight_lbs` (Withings same morning), `navel_waist_in`  
- Notes: “protocol AM post-void” / anything off (sick, huge salt day, travel)

## Do not
- Compare to old **36"** estimate (retired)  
- Use waistband / “thickest butt vertical” / hips as navel SoT  
- Panic on **±0.25"** week-to-week noise — trend over **2–4 weeks**  
- Change calories from one tape alone if adherence was fine  

## Quick checklist (print / save)
- [ ] AM · post-void · fasted  
- [ ] Same tape · bare skin · through **navel** · horizontal  
- [ ] Snug not crushing · normal exhale  
- [ ] Two reads · average · log + send  

## Other sites (optional)
Same AM conditions. Thighs: mid-point inguinal crease → patella, both legs, tape parallel to floor (remeasure taught the left-thigh error). Chest: nipple-line horizontal. Hips: fullest glute **horizontal**. Protocol detail for those can stay lighter — **navel is the Phase gate.**
