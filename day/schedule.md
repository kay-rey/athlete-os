## 7-day ops

### Monday — Errands + Flow teach ± MT
Wake ≈11 → scale → Gate 1 → errands (untracked NEAT — no step goal) → sertraline 3:00 → Gate 4 → Flow PM (Adults = white-belt room, light contact) → **optional MT** (best night) → Gate 6. No lift / no GB.

### Tuesday — Lift A + Flow teach
Wake ≈11 → collagen → Gate 1 (+30g carbs) → **Lift A 11:45–12:33** → sertraline → Gate 4 → Flow PM Adults (light / white-belt room). No GB / no MT.

### Wednesday — Flow AM → energy fork → Flow PM ± MT
Wake ≈8 (AM teach) → Gate 1 → Flow AM.  
**Green:** GB **10:45 GB3 No-Gi** or **12:00 + 1:00 open mat** (2× credit) → **no MT**.  
**Red (tired):** skip GB → Flow PM → **MT optional** (technical only) → chase 2× Sat.  
GB day: WPI+creatine post; Zone 2 deferred to Sunday.

### Thursday — GB hard midday + Flow teach
Wake ≈11 → Gate 1 → clamp → **GB 12:00–1:00** (+ **1:00–1:30** GB3 Open Mat if possible) locked 2× credit → WPI+creatine → sertraline → Gate 4 → Flow PM Adults (still light / white-belt room — not a second hard session). No MT.

### Friday — Lift B + free evening
**Lift B 11:45–12:33**. Prefer **no GB**. Last-resort 2×: **11:00 Comp** before lift or **6:00** No-Gi after — see `calendar/academies.md`. Gate 6 dinner / family.

### Saturday — Flow AM + cleanup → GB if Wed missed
Flow AM teach → mat cleanup. GB **12:00–1:00 only if Wed skipped**. Else done for 2× with Thu.

### Sunday — Off mats
Audit weigh-in + waist → log `journal/anthro.csv` → Gate 1 → **Zone 2 45 min @ 125–138** → groceries / sleep bank. Milpa carbs.

## Weekly groceries
Shop **Costco / Sam’s Club** first (you have both), then normal grocery for anything missing. Flavors/avoid → `nutrition/preferences.md`. Chili → `nutrition/recipes/turkey_chili.md`.

- 3.5 lbs chicken · 3.5 lbs turkey/flank/sirloin (chili default **99/1 turkey**; swap **96/4 beef** OK; avoid 80/20 chili) · 2 lbs white fish/tuna
- 2 dozen eggs · 2 cartons egg whites · 2×32oz 0% lactose-free Greek yogurt
- 8 cans black/pinto beans · 2 packs nixtamalized tortillas · chia · squash
- 5–6 avocados · tomatoes/tomatillos/onion/cilantro/peppers (salsa/fajita) · spinach · bananas · honey · salted rice cakes
- **Chili staples:** chili seasoning packets · canned diced tomatoes (**no salt added** preferred) · extra cumin/chili powder/garlic powder to stretch mix without double sodium
- **Tired-night backups** (wife wiped / no cook): rotisserie chicken · frozen cooked chicken strips or lean meatballs · extra canned beans · 1–2 lower-sodium canned soups to *upgrade* with chicken — see `nutrition/preferences.md` fallbacks
- Condiments to keep: salsa, guac fixings, light mayo or crema, tomato-style BBQ — **do not buy pickles or mustard as staples**
- Drinks: **Fresca** (classic 0-cal; often warehouse multipacks) OK staple; optional sugar-free shaved-ice / Torani SF syrup
- Dispensary: `stack/supplements.md` (Kirkland D3 / multi / fish oil already match Costco)
