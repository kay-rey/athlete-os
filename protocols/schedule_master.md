## 7 Daily Adherence Gates

### Gate 1 (07:30 AM Breakfast)
45g protein (3 whole pasture eggs + 200g egg whites w/ spinach/salsa, **OR** 250g 0% lactose-free Greek yogurt + 1 scoop Whey Isolate) + D3 (2,000–5,000 IU)/K2 (100–180 mcg) + half-dose Omega-3s (1,000–1,500mg EPA/DHA).

### Gate 2 (12:10 PM Pre-Lunch Fiber)
5g unflavored psyllium husk (2.5g in Wk 1) in 12 oz cold water + 6 oz water chaser (15–20 min pre-lunch).

### Gate 3 (12:30 PM Lunch)
50g protein (6.5 oz / 185g cooked chicken breast or flank steak) + 150g cooked black beans + fajita veggies + **1 tablet Kirkland Signature Daily Multi** (taken strictly at lunch with solid food/fat; separates 18mg iron & 200mg calcium by 9 hours from evening magnesium).

### Gate 4 (04:30 PM Pre-Mat Anchor)
25g protein (1 scoop zero-lactose Whey Isolate in water or 4 oz turkey breast) → crosses the mandatory ≥115g daytime protein threshold before 5:00 PM.

### Gate 5 (Sparring Days Only — 4-Step Peri-Sparring Anti-Binge Loop)

1. **T−45m Pre-Sparring (Glycemic Clamp):** 35–45g fast, low-fiber carbs + 500–750mg sodium in 16 oz water (1 large ripe banana + 1 tbsp honey + sea salt, **OR** 2 salted rice cakes + 1.5 tbsp honey/jam) to preserve kimono grip strength endurance and clamp blood glucose.
2. **T+15m Post-Roll (Locker-Room Brake):** 30g Whey Isolate + 5g Creatine Monohydrate in 12 oz cold water before driving home.
3. **T+45m Post-Roll (Intestinal Gel Pre-Load):** 5g psyllium husk in 12 oz water upon arriving home before showering.
4. **T+65m Post-Roll (Milpa Dinner):** 50g lean protein (7 oz chicken/steak/fish) + 175–200g cooked black beans (~40g slow carbs) + 200g roasted veggies + 50g avocado + second half-dose Omega-3s.

### Gate 6 (08:10 PM Non-Sparring Dinner)
5g psyllium husk at 8:10 PM, followed by Milpa Dinner + half-dose Omega-3s at 8:30 PM (add 5g Creatine if non-sparring day).

### Gate 7 (09:30 PM Sleep Architecture)
200–300mg elemental Magnesium Bisglycinate + 144mg elemental Magnesium L-Threonate 60 min pre-bed; kitchen closed.
